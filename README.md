About me
I was born and raised in Rourkela, Odisha, and went to school here through +2 Science. I'm now studying Mechatronics, which brings together mechanical, electrical and control-systems fundamentals.

I'm comfortable on both sides of automation. On the software side I work with PLCs and AutoCAD. On the hardware side I work with CNC, lathe, milling and drilling machines.

In a one-month PLC internship at CTTC Bhubaneswar, I used TIA Portal and Simatic Manager to build ladder logic for control sequences. I also learned to configure, test and troubleshoot PLC-based control panels under guided supervision.

I'm disciplined, punctual and quick to pick up new tools, and I see project work through from start to finish. Outside of study I go to the gym, read and travel.
